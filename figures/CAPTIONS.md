# Figure captions

Figure 1. Unweighted sequential selection counts. Revised primary eligibility admits medication-confirmed cases without BP; BP completeness is a sensitivity. The classifiable stage includes only records with resolved operational status. The last two stages distinguish outcome/exposure completeness from covariate completeness.

Figure 2. Survey-weighted awareness in the primary hypertension domain by current insurance. Error bars are 95% beta/Korn–Graubard-type intervals from R survey; the plotted insurance estimates meet numeric NCHS screening. Unweighted n is outcome-specific. These are unadjusted percentages.

Figure 3. Model 3 jointly adjusts for insurance, age, sex, six race/ethnicity categories, four education categories, and continuous PIR. Intervals use R svyglm residual design df (3). Covariate estimates are model coefficients, not identified causal effects; ORs are not risk ratios.

Figure 4. Model 3 predictions averaged over the weighted complete-case covariate distribution after setting insurance to each status. Joint Taylor linearization includes coefficient and empirical-distribution uncertainty; primary marginal intervals use Model 3 residual df, matching coefficient inference, with bounded logit probability intervals. Descriptive model standardization does not identify an insurance intervention effect.

Figure 5. Selected secondary estimates, including reviewer-directed eligibility revisions; no multiplicity adjustment or significance-driven selection. Case definitions alter populations. The measured-only 140/90 point estimate reverses direction, with substantial uncertainty. Each interval uses its model-specific residual design df.
